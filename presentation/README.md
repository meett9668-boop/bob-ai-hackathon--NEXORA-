Recommended Slide Structure (5–8 slides)

Slide 1: Title — NEXORA — AI-Powered Power Grid Intelligence
Team: NEXORA
Track: AI

Slide 2: Problem — Power Outage Prediction & Grid Equipment Failure Advisor
Power distribution networks depend on critical equipment such as
transformers, substations and feeders. Unexpected failures and
outages can interrupt service, increase maintenance effort,
damage equipment and reduce grid reliability.

         Who has it?
         Grid operators, maintenance teams, field engineers, utilities
         and electricity consumers.

         Why does it matter?
         Operators need earlier visibility into abnormal conditions so
         high-risk assets can be identified and preventive action can be
         considered before failures escalate.

Slide 3: Solution — NEXORA
NEXORA is an AI-powered grid intelligence platform that analyzes
grid and equipment conditions to detect abnormalities, estimate
outage and equipment-failure risk, assess asset health, generate
alerts and provide preventive guidance.

         Monitor
         ↓
         Detect
         ↓
         Predict
         ↓
         Assess
         ↓
         Explain
         ↓
         Recommend
         ↓
         Display
